# Microgame: [DuckDuck](MobileGameProject\Assets\Scripts\Microgames\MG1_DuckDuck)

## Concept
You are a duck and you are being hunted! Avoid getting shot until the hunters run out of ammo!

## Player Action
The player swipes in the direction they want to flap. Swiping in the same direction multiple times makes the player move faster. The player bounces off the sides.

## Success / Failure
**Success condition:**  The player avoids getting shot until the hunters run out of ammo.
**Failure condition:**  The player gets shot, caught in a shot-zone.
